EU wine package: rules and dates for winegrowers

Regulation (EU) 2026/471 entered into force on 18 March 2026. Some measures apply later: check the provision and the circumstances of your authorisation or product before acting.

Planting authorisations

The regime becomes permanent and the validity of the replanting authorisations concerned extends to eight years. The waiver without penalties for qualifying authorisations granted before 1 January 2025 must be notified before the authorisation expires, and no later than 31 December 2026. The year-end deadline does not revive an expired authorisation.

Dealcoholised wines: two different thresholds

The new “alcohol-free” wording covers the relevant categories at no more than 0.5% vol.; the additional “0.0%” wording uses a 0.05% vol. ceiling. “Reduced alcohol” requires more than 0.5% vol. and at least a 30% reduction from the minimum strength of the category before dealcoholisation.

These new labelling provisions apply from 19 September 2027 under Article 1(10)(a), as corrected on 22 May 2026. Transitional stock rules also apply. Check the exact product and production date for ingredient and nutrition obligations; vintage alone is insufficient.

Check each authorisation’s grant and expiry dates with the competent body. For a label, distinguish the current rules from the future framework and have the exact product and destination reviewed.

Official sources : UE 2026/471 · 22/05/2026
