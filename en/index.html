Who we are

About

Mon Chai was born from a simple observation: wine estates still juggle too often between tools that do not communicate, with much reskilling and little overall visibility.

After several years in wine management software, Denis designed Mon Chai to link the main activities of a domain within a single tool, from vine to sale.

Laura then joined to develop user experience, design, communication and support of the fields.

Today, our beta-test program allows us to confront Mon Chai with the field and to co-build the most useful developments.

Our ambition: to build a useful, clear and adapted tool to the reality of the fields.

🇫🇷 Developed and hosted in France
