Vineyard & plant protection

From certiphyto to the mandatory numerical plant register in 2027, the winery is the most regulated field in the field: each treatment is registered, each dose is justified, and CAP aids depend on the quality of your records. This category follows the plant protection obligations of the winemaker — the plant register, IFT, crop book, environmental certification — with deadlines to be adopted and concrete advice to stay in order without spending his evenings.
