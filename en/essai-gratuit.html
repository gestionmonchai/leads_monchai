Try Mon Chai for free

You create your account, you test the wine-growing software on your domain data, and then you decide. Without credit card, no commitment, no automatic restart.

  • Without credit cardNo payment requested at the creation of the account.
  • No commitmentThe subscription is monthly and stops when you decide.

Free until 31 October 2026

Mon Chai is in beta-test program until October 31, 2026. During this period, access to the software is free, without means of payment, and the transfer of your data as well as training are included. In return, we ask you for your feedback: they are the ones who decide what we build next.

The domains that join the beta test keep the launch price at 49 € tax excl. per month for a year. Details are on the page Pricing.

Then 30 days free trial, without credit card

From 1ir November 2026, the creation of an account opens 30 days free trial. No payment is required to start. At the end of 30 days, nothing is triggered by itself: since no card has been registered, nothing can be taken. You choose to continue or stop.

What the test includes

The test gives access to the complete software, not a reduced version:

  • Wine and harvest: plots, work log, compliant digital plant protection register 2027, inputs synchronized with the marketing authorisations of Anses.
  • Cellar and winemaking: bowling, technical batches, operations, analyses.
  • Stocks and traceability, packaging and storage.
  • Sales, customers and billing.
  • DRM and compliance: cellar register, material accounting, data ready for DTI+ portal.
  • Wine tourism and sales in the cellar.
  • IA Assistant in French, operated on our servers in France.

The complete list is on the page All features.

Free wineware, does it exist?

Yes, as long as you know what you get and what you lose. The spreadsheet is free, and it does real services as long as the domain remains small. Some paid software also offers a free version limited in volumes or modules.

The difficulty comes with obligations. The phytosanitary register becomes numerical on 1ir January 2027 and must export in a specific format. The material accounting that feeds the DRM assumes that the inputs, outputs and losses are linked to the same lots. Traceability requires finding a bottle to its plot. These are data linked to each other, and a binder rarely keeps them long without double entry.

Mon Chai is not a free software. It is free to try, on your own data, as long as the beta-test lasts, then 30 days. It is the only honest way to know if it saves you time.

Frequently asked questions about free trial

Is Mon Chai free?

Until October 31, 2026, yes: the beta-test program gives access to all software without paying and without payment. Then, Mon Chai is a paid software, 49 € tax excl. per month, with 30 days free trial to test it on your own data.

Do you need a credit card to try Mon Chai?

No. The account creation does not require any means of payment. You enter your name, domain and email address, and you start working.

What happens at the end of the free trial?

No automatic credit cards were requested, so nothing can be taken. You freely choose to continue or stop, and you leave with your data.

Is there a commitment or hidden costs?

No. Subscription is monthly and can be terminated at any time. Only training and data recovery are charged separately, as an option, and always announced before. Details are on the page Pricing.

Can we get his data back if we stop?

Yes. Your data is yours and is exported to CSV and PDF at any time, free of charge, without having to ask.

Is there a free wineware?

There are free tools, starting with the spreadsheet, and limited versions of paid software. They are kept as long as the domain remains small and the obligations remain simple.ir January 2027, the material accounting and traceability of lots require data linked to each other, which a spreadsheet does not guarantee. Mon Chai is not free, but it tries for free, on your data, before any decision is made.
